Place Micro / Animal on any empty cell.

Game ends when the 6×6 board is full. Merges free up space, so keep evolving!

How to play
  • Board starts with 3× M (1 pt), 2× A (2 pts), 1× H (5 pts) on random cells.
  • Click an empty cell → a random M or A spawns.
  • If an orthogonal neighbour (up/down/left/right) is the same type, they merge into the next stage: M+M → A, A+A → H.
  • H is final and never merges.
  • Score = sum of all cells (M=1, A=2, H=5).

Faithful port of Oyun.py (Kivy) game logic, including random merge survivor and chained merges.
